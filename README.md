## From learning to a next step

Murshid connects short-form learning with mentors, communities, events, and
opportunities. The product hypothesis is simple: a useful idea should lead
to something a person can do, not just more content to consume.

- **Discover:** explore relevant learning, save resources, and find opportunities.
- **Get guidance:** find a mentor, book a session, and prepare a useful conversation.
- **Act:** join an event or community and organize the next step.

The MVP includes an operator console for mentor review, moderation, and
catalog curation. Arabic, English, and right-to-left layouts are part of the
product foundation.
